About Me

I'm a Senior SDET who owns quality end to end, from reading the PRD and writing Gherkin scenarios to building Selenium WebDriver and Rest Assured automation that runs release after release.

At Recrosoft, I test Keychain, an AI-powered platform that connects CPG brands with manufacturers. I plan and test new features on Staging, automate them, and run regression on UAT twice a week before releases go to Production.
